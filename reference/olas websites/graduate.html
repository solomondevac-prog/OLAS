Research & Faculty

Graduate study is defined by discovery. Our faculty are at the forefront of global research, securing millions in grants annually. As a graduate student, you'll have unparalleled opportunities to contribute to projects that solve real-world problems and advance human knowledge.

Application Requirements & Deadlines

The Graduate School accepts applications on a rolling basis, but priority deadlines apply to specific departments and for scholarship consideration. We require a bachelor's degree, a statement of purpose, three letters of recommendation, and in some cases, standardized test scores (e.g., GRE/GMAT).
