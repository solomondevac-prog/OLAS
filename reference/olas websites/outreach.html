Student Support Services

We believe that comprehensive student support is key to academic success and personal well-being. Our dedicated team provides confidential **counseling, academic advising, and wellness programs** to all enrolled students. Services are available throughout the academic year and are free of charge.

Core Support Areas:

  • Mental Health Counseling and Crisis Intervention
  • College and Career Planning
  • Academic Tutoring and Study Skills Workshops
  • Disability Support Services

Local Community Outreach Programs

Our school is committed to being an active partner in the local community. Through volunteer programs and resource sharing, we aim to address local needs and enrich the lives of our neighbors. These efforts provide students with invaluable real-world experience and foster civic responsibility.

**Featured Program:** The **Weekend Food Backpack Program**—run by student volunteers—provides meals for local families during school holidays. Additionally, our campus hosts free adult literacy classes every Saturday morning.

Resource & Training Center

The center serves as a hub for both students and the public, offering access to specialized equipment, software, and training workshops. Our goal is to bridge the digital divide and provide professional development opportunities for educators and community members alike.

**Upcoming Workshops:** Free seminars on digital marketing, resume building, and basic coding skills are scheduled monthly.
